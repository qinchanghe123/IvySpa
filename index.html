Frequently Asked Questions

When should I arrive for my appointment?

We recommend arriving 10-15 minutes prior to your scheduled appointment time. This gives you time to fill out any necessary forms and start relaxing before your session.

Can I get a massage if I have an injury?

Yes, but please inform us when booking and tell your therapist before the session starts. We can adjust the pressure and avoid specific areas to ensure your safety.

Is there parking available?

Yes, we have a dedicated parking lot right in front of the building (Ste G). It is free and spacious.

What is your cancellation policy?

We kindly ask for at least 24 hours notice if you need to cancel or reschedule. Cancellations made within less than 24 hours may be subject to a fee.
